# Cade Clonts

**Electrical engineer — embedded hardware, power electronics, and controls.**
Phoenix, Arizona.

I like building things that have to work in the real world: boards that survive being plugged in wrong, control loops that behave when the input is noisy, and power systems sized with actual margin. Most of my projects start on a bench and end with a fabricated board, a running system, and a list of what I'd change in the next revision.

I hold a B.S. in Electrical Systems Engineering from Arizona State University and am finishing an accelerated M.S. there (expected Spring 2027), with graduate coursework in power electronic converters, applied photovoltaics, and multimodal ML for engineering applications. My interests sit at the intersection of **power and energy**, **controls and automation**, and **defense electronics**, and I'm looking for internship or full-time roles in those areas.

## What I work with

| Area | Tools and skills |
|---|---|
| **PCB and hardware design** | Altium Designer, KiCad, SMT assembly and rework, board bring-up, power-rail design (buck, LDO), BOM and vendor sourcing |
| **Embedded firmware** | ESP32 (MicroPython, ESP-IDF), PIC (MPLAB/MCC), UART / I2C / SPI, MQTT over TLS, async event loops |
| **Power and energy** | Converter design and loss analysis, battery management, microgrid modeling (Xendee), PV system design |
| **Controls and modeling** | MATLAB / Simulink, digital filter design, vehicle dynamics simulation, ROS |
| **Fabrication** | 3D printing (FDM), CAD (STEP/enclosure design), sheet-metal fabrication, hand and shop tools |
